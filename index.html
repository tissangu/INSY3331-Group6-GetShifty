How GetShifty Responds to Call-Outs

The Small-Business Manager Challenge

When an employee calls out sick for Friday evening, managers traditionally spend 45 minutes manually texting staff and checking who is free, who already worked 40 hours, and who has the right role.

How to test it in GetShifty:

  1. Click "Load Demo Scenario" in the top header (creates 10 employees & a full week of shifts).
  2. Click "Generate Schedule". You will see all 7 days staffed.
  3. Find Friday Evening. Notice assigned staff (e.g. Marcus Vance).
  4. Click the small [Call-Out] icon next to an assigned employee.
  5. Confirm the call-out. The shift will be flagged as vacated.
  6. Click "Regenerate Schedule". GetShifty will find the best eligible replacement (e.g. Liam O'Connor) while keeping all other shifts frozen!

⚖️ Responsible AI & Labor Compliance Notice: GetShifty utilizes deterministic heuristic optimization to schedule shifts based on entered constraints. Fairness is treated as an optimization objective, not an absolute guarantee. Managers remain responsible for ensuring schedules comply with applicable federal, state, and local labor regulations, mandatory rest breaks, minor working hour limits, overtime compensation, and collective bargaining agreements.
